## Scope and mode
Persuade. Rebuild the seven existing service cards, retaining the pinned stack, catalogue meaning, EZWAY identity and exact chapter colours. Implementation is code-led under the user's detailed brief; no new brand/world or unrelated section changes.

## Audience, job and action
Business owners compare ways to present their business, connect an offline visit and get support. Demonstrations explain the service; actual controls let people explore the menu and the existing catalogue remains the source of commercial truth. EasyChat must remain explicitly in development.

## First viewport
Keep the existing section heading and lead. A broad pink first card combines left-aligned Google Sans copy and an architectural website assembled from navigation, imagery and service blocks. The preview has a fixed geometry, ample breathing room and a subtly offset mobile crop. The colour itself is the surface; no decorative glass or fake metrics.

## Composition and content
Distinct compositions: institutional browser build; cream menu app and food crops; black NFC plate/phone interaction; lavender editorial project collage; yellow landing-page journey; blue conceptual chat with document review; lime support conversation and resolution checklist. Use existing assets, real Lucide icons and catalogue copy. Never introduce fabricated clients, growth claims, conversion percentages or availability.

## Signature interaction
One reversible GSAP timeline owns the stack. Each chapter's interior has a separate subtree and a service-specific child timeline, sampled by that same scroll playhead. Entry, demonstration and exit reverse naturally; no CSS keyframe restart on data-active, no React state updates per scroll frame, no geometry reads/writes in the update loop. Native reduced motion presents the same seven cards in normal document flow, with static completed demonstrations.

## Validation and boundaries
Inspect every card at desktop and phone sizes, plus short landscape. Check bidirectional/fast scroll, fixed preview geometry at the same progress, all controls, keyboard focus, native reduced motion, no-JS fallback, palette, text contrast and overflow. Run build, TypeScript, scoped lint and meaningful browser tests. Preserve existing DESIGN.md and the hero/management changes.
