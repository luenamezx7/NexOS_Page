# EZWAY — Além da gestão

Scoped product context from the user's redesign request and `src/config/solutions.ts`.

## Platform and audience
Responsive web section within the existing Next.js homepage. Business owners and professionals compare ways to present their work, organise access to information, and discuss a delivery with the EZWAY team.

## Product truth
The public catalogue is the authority for names, needs, availability and commercial limitations. Preserve these seven chapters: institutional sites, digital menus, NFC plates with QR Code, portfolios, landing pages, EasyChat, and support. Demonstrations are illustrative; they do not promise checkout, automatic integrations, contractual service levels or guaranteed results.

## Confirmed commitments
Preserve the existing EZWAY identity, Google Sans and chapter colours: pink #ff78bb, lime #b9ef75, OLED black, lavender #d1c5f1, yellow #f6e8a9 and blue #b9dcf3. EasyChat remains explicitly in development and document output requires review before sharing. No invented metrics, clients, growth percentages or conversion claims.

## Experience and accessibility
Keep the scroll stack and give each chapter its own service demonstration. Movement must reverse naturally and stay stable through fast scroll direction changes. Native reduced motion presents the catalogue in normal document flow with static demonstrations. Keyboard focus reveals the corresponding card; menu categories support pointer and keyboard input. The complete catalogue remains readable without JavaScript.

## Boundaries
Only the ecosystem section and its owned stack/demo components change. Existing hero, management, authentication and global DESIGN.md stay intact. The card palette is a retained commitment, not a new site-wide design system.
