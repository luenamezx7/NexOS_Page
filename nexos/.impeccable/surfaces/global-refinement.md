Use existing GSAP for scroll scenes and Motion for local transitions. Individual CSS scale composes safely with transform owners on shared landing actions, excluding hero and approved cards. Diagrams link actual solution anchors and retain the existing AnimatedBeam.

## Validation contract
Check 320,375,390,430,768,1024,1280,1440,1920,2560 px; short landscapes via approved-stack tests. Inspect normal/reduced motion and live preference changes, keyboard NFC dialogs, focus return, menus, CTAs, FAQ and reverse scroll. Build, TypeScript, lint, unit/security/entry and targeted browser regressions. Profile locally and distinguish emulation from physical devices; do not claim universal 60 fps or WCAG conformance from an automated score.

Future general-purpose (two or three colours) and menu plate designs will be supplied later; do not invent them or alter the approved NFC card in Além da gestão.
